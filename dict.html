Official slang, terminology, and definitions used in the Kobosh Joei Truth community.

koboshnoun

The official name of the "Kobosh Joei Truth".

"Kobosh" is a term used in the context of the Kobosh Joei Truth.

koboshverb

Depending on the context, to finish quickly, or to "lock in".

"I koboshed this task in no time." / "I need to kobosh so I can finish this project."

koboshadjective

Something that is exceptionally good, or someone that is smart, successful, or skilled.

"That was a kobosh move!" / "You are so kobosh!"

koboshernoun / adjective

Someone who is bad at something, not smart, unsuccessful, or lacking skill.

"This kobosher is not going to make it." / "Don't be such a kobosher, you can do better, kobosh!"

koboshiannoun

A formal member of the Kobosh Joei Truth community.

"This koboshian is really dedicated to the cause." / "Koboshians are known for their commitment and skill."

joeinoun

A group, clan, or organization.

"The Kobosh Joei Truth is a group of people who are dedicated to the cause."

joeiverb

To gather people together, or to unite/recruit.

"We need to joei more members to expand our footprint."

crowbarnoun

An amount of money equivalent to approximately fifteen dollars ($15).

"This item is worth a crowbar." / "Can you lend me a crowbar?"

connavon / connavannoun

Teens of Persian descent that frequently hang out in local McDonald's outlets. Connavon (plural: connavonts) refers to males, while connavan (plural: connavants) refers to females.

"The connavonts are always hanging out at that McDonald's after school."

condavon / condavannoun

Subcultural slang for gangsters or drug addicts. Condavon (plural: condavonts) refers to males, while condavan (plural: condavants) refers to females.

"The condavants were seen last time hanging around downtown."
